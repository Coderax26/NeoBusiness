**Lightweight ERP/CRM Architecture & Operational Transformation Plan**

**Subject:** End-to-End Digital Operations Blueprint for Multi-Site Food Trading & Processed Food Production

## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY & CASE STUDY SYNTHESIS

The Neo Project represents an operationally active, multi-site business managed by Mr. X across two tightly coupled value streams: **Food Trading** (buying and selling food commodities) and **Processed Food Production** (manufacturing finished goods at a factory supported by 10 to 15 employees). Logistics and field activities are carried out by approximately 3 to 4 drivers alongside dedicated delivery and collection staff.

A comprehensive review of the field study reveals a classic growth bottleneck: the business has outgrown personal, phone-based coordination, making the owner the primary constraint to scale. Mr. X currently acts as a live human router for customer price negotiations, order capture, supplier price/shortage resolution, factory production prioritization, driver dispatch, field troubleshooting, and end-of-day manual account reconciliation. Because operational state resides in phone calls, personal memory, and fragmented manual notes, any change in one stage (such as a supplier price hike or quantity shortfall) triggers a cascade of reactive phone calls across multiple functions.

### The Strategic Solution: "NeoCore" Lightweight ERP/CRM

The solution is not to add administrative headcount or deploy a bloated, rigid legacy ERP. Instead, Neo requires **NeoCore**—a mobile-first, lightweight, exception-driven ERP/CRM tailored for fast-moving food trading and batch food processing.

**Core Transformation Objectives:**

1. **Shift Mr. X from "Live Router" to "Exception Approver":** Routine orders, standard pricing, dispatch assignments, and payment logging are executed directly by staff within strict system guardrails. Mr. X only intervenes via one-tap mobile approvals when margin thresholds, credit limits, or supplier shortages breach defined tolerances.


2. **Eliminate Verbal State:** Convert transient phone conversations into structured, real-time digital records (Quotes, Sales Orders, Purchase Orders, Production Batches, Trip Sheets, and Collection Receipts).


3. **Close the Financial Loop:** Replace error-prone end-of-day manual account reviews with real-time field collection capture, automated ledger posting, and daily cash/bank reconciliation.

## 2. CONFIRMED FACTS, ASSUMPTIONS & OPEN QUESTIONS

To maintain strict engineering and consulting rigor, the system design separates verified observations from the field study, architectural assumptions required to build a complete blueprint, and discovery questions required before code freeze.

### 2.1 Confirmed Facts (Derived Directly from Case Study)

* **Dual Business Model:** The company operates both a food trading division and a processed food manufacturing division that share customers, suppliers, and logistics.


* **Multi-Site Footprint:** Operations span multiple physical locations (including a manufacturing factory, trading storage/warehouses, and administrative points).


* **Workforce Structure:** Headed by Mr. X, supported by a Production Manager, 10–15 factory employees, 3–4 drivers, and dedicated delivery/collection staff.


* **Communication Bottleneck:** Mr. X handles high daily call volumes covering customer price negotiations, order taking, supplier price/quantity negotiations, production scheduling, driver directions, and payment follow-ups.


* **Supplier Volatility:** Suppliers frequently change prices or fail to fulfill agreed quantities, directly impacting customer commitments and margins.


* **Financial Vulnerability:** Collections are followed up by staff and Mr. X, with accounts checked manually at the end of the day—creating a documented risk of missed financial entries and incomplete receivable tracking.



### 2.2 Working Architectural Assumptions

* **A1 (Product Nature):** Food items and raw materials are subject to shelf-life expiration, batch/lot tracking requirements, and variable weight/packaging units (e.g., kg, crates, cartons, metric tons).
* **A2 (Device Availability):** Field drivers, collection agents, and the Production Manager have access to entry-level Android smartphones with intermittent 4G/5G connectivity, while office/sales staff have access to desktop browsers or tablets.
* **A3 (Pricing Model):** Because food trading prices fluctuate daily based on supplier costs, the business uses a **Daily Base Price List** with allowed negotiation bands (e.g., Floor Price vs. Target Price) rather than static annual catalog prices.
* **A4 (Payment Modes):** Collections involve a mix of physical cash, bank transfers (NEFT/RTGS/IMPS/UPI), and post-dated checks collected in the field by drivers or collection staff.
* **A5 (Tax & Compliance):** The system must support standard regional tax invoicing (e.g., GST/VAT calculation, HSN/SAC codes, and e-Way bill/delivery challan generation for goods in transit).

## 4. PRACTICAL FUTURE-STATE TARGET OPERATING MODEL (TOM)

The future-state operating model reorganizes the business from a **Hub-and-Spoke (Owner-Centric)** model to a **Tiered Process-Driven Model** governed by **Management by Exception**.

```
FUTURE STATE: "NEOCORE" CENTRALIZED SYSTEM OF RECORD
====================================================

 [Customers] ---> [Sales & Dispatch Coordinator] (Handles 85% Routine Orders/Routes)
                         |
                         v
            +-------------------------+
            |   NEOCORE CLOUD ERP     | <--- Real-Time State, ATP Inventory,
            | (Single Source of Truth)|      Automated Ledgers & Audit Trail
            +-------------------------+
             /        |        |      \
            v         v        v       v
    [Prod. Mgr]  [Drivers] [Collectors] [MR. X - OWNER DASHBOARD]
   (Work Orders)  (ePOD)   (Receipts)   (Handles ONLY the 15% Exceptions:
                                         Price Overrides, Credit Breaches,
                                         Supplier Shortfalls, EOD Sign-off)

```

### 4.1 The Three Operational Layers

1. **Layer 1: Standard Execution (Automated & Staff-Led — ~85% of daily volume)**
* A designated **Operations / Sales Coordinator** (existing administrative/collection staff upskilled, not necessariamente a new hire) answers routine customer order calls, enters quantities into NeoCore, and quotes prices within Mr. X’s pre-approved daily price band.


* The **Production Manager** views confirmed demand on a factory tablet, executes production batches, and logs finished output without calling Mr. X.


* **Drivers & Collection Staff** view their daily assigned stops, delivery quantities, and collection targets on their mobile app, capturing deliveries and payments digitally.




2. **Layer 2: Exception Escalation (System-Routed — ~15% of daily volume)**
* Whenever a transaction violates a business rule (e.g., customer requests a 5% discount below floor price, customer exceeds credit limit, supplier hikes price by >2%, or supplier short-ships by 20%), NeoCore pauses the transaction and pushes an actionable **Exception Card** to Mr. X’s smartphone.




3. **Layer 3: Strategic Control & Governance (Owner-Led — Mr. X)**
* **Morning (15 mins):** Mr. X updates the **Daily Base Price & Floor Sheet** based on market rates and reviews the day's Production & Dispatch capacity.
* **Daytime (On-Demand via App):** Mr. X approves/rejects Exception Cards with a single tap (Approve / Counter-Offer / Reject) instead of fielding 50+ unstructured phone calls.


* **Evening (15 mins):** Instead of manually hunting for missed entries, Mr. X reviews the **Automated EOD Reconciliation Dashboard**, verifying that physical cash handed over to the cashier matches the digital receipts logged by field staff.

### 5.3 Core Business Rules & Automated Guardrails

To eliminate routine phone calls to Mr. X while protecting profitability, NeoCore enforces 8 hard coded business rules:

* **BR-01: Dynamic Pricing Band Rule:** Every product has a daily `Target_Price` and `Floor_Price` (auto-calculated as `Latest_Landed_Cost + Min_Margin_%`, or manually set by Mr. X). Orders priced $\ge$ `Floor_Price` auto-approve. Orders priced $<$ `Floor_Price` enter `PENDING_OWNER_APPROVAL` status.
* **BR-02: Automated Credit Control Rule:** Every customer has a `Credit_Limit` and `Max_Overdue_Days` (e.g., ₹1,00,000 and 15 days). If `Current_AR_Balance + New_Order_Value > Credit_Limit` OR any invoice is overdue beyond `Max_Overdue_Days`, the system blocks dispatch and triggers a **Credit Hold Exception** to Mr. X (or requires Cash-on-Delivery).
* **BR-03: Supplier Price Variance Rule:** If a supplier quotes a price on a PO that exceeds the `Last_Purchase_Price` by more than $X\%$ (e.g., 2%), the PO is locked for Mr. X's approval, and the system automatically flags all open customer quotes/orders for that item whose margin would drop below threshold.


* **BR-04: Hard Inventory Allocation (ATP):** Confirming a Sales Order immediately places a soft lock on available stock at the designated site (`Available_Stock = Physical_Stock - Committed_Stock`). For processed foods with insufficient finished stock, the deficit automatically generates a `Production_Requisition` for the Production Manager.


* **BR-05: Blind Receiving & Shortfall Rule:** Warehouse staff receiving goods from suppliers enter actual physical quantities received without seeing the PO quantity (Blind GRN). If `Received_Qty < PO_Qty`, the system automatically flags a **Supplier Shortfall Exception**.


* **BR-06: Production Yield Tolerance Rule:** When closing a factory Work Order, if `Actual_Output + Recorded_Wastage` deviates from `Standard_BOM_Input` by more than $\pm 3\%$, the system flags an **Abnormal Yield Variance** alert to Mr. X while allowing finished goods to enter stock.
* **BR-07: Mobile Custody Chain for Cash (Zero Missed Entries):** When a Driver or Collection Agent logs a cash collection of ₹10,000 in the field, two entries happen simultaneously:
1. Customer AR Balance decreases by ₹10,000 (Customer gets instant SMS/WhatsApp receipt).
2. Field Agent's **Virtual Wallet (Cash-in-Transit)** increases by ₹10,000.
The agent's wallet is only cleared when they physically hand over the cash at the office at EOD and the Cashier/Mr. X clicks **"Verify Cash Handover."** It is mathematically impossible for an entry to be forgotten at the end of the day.




* **BR-08: Mandatory ePOD Closure:** A trip cannot be closed by a driver until every invoice on the manifest is marked as `Delivered (Full)`, `Delivered (Partial - with returned qty logged)`, or `Undelivered (with reason code)`.

### 5.4 Scenario-Based Exception Workflows (Addressing Case Study Scenarios A–F)

The case study highlights six specific operational scenarios (Scenarios A through F). Below is how NeoCore resolves each scenario systematically:

#### Scenario A: Customer Negotiates a Price



1. Customer calls the Sales Desk (or Mr. X) requesting 500 kg of Item Y at ₹92/kg.


2. Staff enters the inquiry into NeoCore. The screen displays: `Latest Cost: ₹82/kg | Floor Price: ₹90/kg | Target Price: ₹95/kg`.
3. **Path 1 (Within Band):** Since ₹92 $\ge$ ₹90 Floor Price, the staff member confirms the order immediately on the call. **Zero calls to Mr. X.**
4. **Path 2 (Below Band):** Customer insists on ₹88/kg. Staff clicks *"Request Price Override."* Mr. X gets a push notification: *"Customer ABC wants 500kg Item Y @ ₹88 (Margin: 7.3%, Floor: ₹90). [Approve ₹88] [Counter ₹89.50] [Reject]."* Mr. X taps `[Counter ₹89.50]` in 3 seconds. Staff relays and locks the order.

#### Scenario B: Supplier Changes Price



1. Supplier informs procurement that raw/traded Material Z has increased from ₹50/kg to ₹56/kg (+12%).


2. Staff enters ₹56/kg into the draft PO. **BR-03** triggers a **Supplier Price Spike Alert** to Mr. X.


3. Mr. X’s approval card shows: *"Supplier X raised Material Z by +12% (₹50 -> ₹56). Impact: 3 Open Customer Orders will drop below 5% margin; Floor Price for Item Z needs adjustment from ₹58 to ₹64."*
4. Mr. X taps *"Approve PO & Auto-Update Floor Price to ₹64"* and selects whether to honor or renegotiate the 3 unconfirmed customer quotes.



#### Scenario C: Supplier Cannot Fulfill Quantity



1. Neo orders 1,000 kg from Supplier A, but Supplier A can only supply 600 kg.


2. Staff logs `Confirmed Qty: 600 kg` on the PO. NeoCore splits the remaining 400 kg into an **Unfulfilled Deficit** and displays ranked backup suppliers (Supplier B @ ₹51/kg, Supplier C @ ₹52/kg) alongside affected customer orders.


3. If backup supply covers the 400 kg within price tolerance, staff issues a secondary PO automatically. If total supply is constrained, Mr. X receives an **Allocation Card** to prioritize which customers receive full delivery vs. partial delivery.



#### Scenario D: Delivery Requires Immediate Attention



1. A driver encounters a vehicle breakdown, road closure, or customer refusal to accept bruised crates during a route.


2. Instead of calling Mr. X, the driver taps **"Report Issue"** on the Driver App (selecting `Customer Partial Rejection`, entering rejected quantity, and snapping a photo).


3. The alert routes to the **Logistics/Ops Coordinator** dashboard first. For routine re-routing or logging standard returns into the credit-note queue, the Coordinator resolves it using pre-set SOPs. Only high-value disputes (e.g., full load rejection) escalate to Mr. X.

#### Scenario E: Customer Payment is Outstanding



1. Every morning at 8:00 AM, NeoCore generates a prioritized **Collection Worklist** for Collection Staff and Drivers based on overdue invoices and scheduled delivery stops.


2. When visiting a customer, the agent views the exact real-time ledger on their phone.
3. If the customer pays cash/check, the agent logs it (**BR-07**), firing an automated WhatsApp receipt to the customer and updating Mr. X's live Cash-in-Transit tracker. If the customer delays payment, the agent must log a mandatory **Promise-to-Pay (PTP) Date & Reason**, which schedules an automated follow-up alert.



#### Scenario F: Production Must Meet an Upcoming Order



1. A confirmed Sales Order requires 200 cartons of Processed Food Item P for delivery tomorrow at 2:00 PM. Current warehouse stock is 50 cartons.


2. NeoCore automatically creates a **Net Production Requirement** of 150 cartons and places it at the top of the Production Manager's tablet queue with a `Required By: Tomorrow 11:00 AM` timestamp.


3. The system checks the BOM raw material stock across sites. If raw materials are available, the Production Manager clicks **"Start Batch,"** allocates the 10–15 factory workers, and clicks **"Complete Batch"** once packaged—automatically notifying Dispatch that the 200 cartons are ready to load.

## 6. END-TO-END FUNCTIONAL COVERAGE ACROSS ALL 9 DOMAINS

### 6.1 Sales & Order Management

* **Quick Order Entry Screen:** Single-screen grid allowing keyboard-fast entry of Customer, Site, Delivery Date, and multiple line items with live stock (ATP) and margin indicators.


* **Quote-to-Order Conversion:** Track verbal/WhatsApp inquiries as `Draft Quotes` with expiration timestamps (e.g., valid for 4 hours given food price volatility) before locking stock as `Confirmed Sales Orders`.


* **Order Lifecycle States:** `Draft` $\rightarrow$ `Pending Approval (if exception)` $\rightarrow$ `Confirmed` $\rightarrow$ `In Production / Sourcing` $\rightarrow$ `Ready for Dispatch` $\rightarrow$ `In Transit` $\rightarrow$ `Delivered (Full/Partial)` $\rightarrow$ `Invoiced & Closed`.



### 6.2 Customer Relationship Management (CRM)

* **360-Degree Customer Profile:** Stores negotiated price tiers, preferred delivery windows, site GPS coordinates, credit limits, payment reliability score, and communication log.


* **Customer Specific Price Memory:** Displays the last 5 prices and quantities sold to that specific customer right on the order screen—eliminating the need to ask Mr. X *"What price did we give them last week?"*.



### 6.3 Supplier Management & Procurement

* **Supplier Rate Card & Volatility Tracker:** Tracks daily/weekly supplier price quotes, historical fill rates (% of ordered quantity actually delivered), and lead times.


* **Back-to-Back Trading POs:** For pure food trading items, allows 1-click generation of a Supplier Purchase Order directly from a Customer Sales Order while displaying net trading spread (₹ and %).



### 6.4 Multi-Site Inventory Management

* **Location-Segregated Stock Ledger:** Tracks inventory separately across Factory Raw Material Store, Factory Finished Goods Store, Trading Warehouses (Site A, Site B), and In-Transit Vehicles.


* **FEFO (First-Expiry, First-Out) & Batch Control:** Tracks perishable food batches by receipt/production date and expiry date, alerting staff when stock approaches shelf-life limits.
* **Dual Unit of Measure (Catch Weight Support):** Supports food items tracked simultaneously by Packaging Unit (Crates/Bags) and Actual Weight (Kilograms).

### 6.5 Factory Production & Light MRP

* **Recipe / Bill of Materials (BOM) Engine:** Defines standard raw material inputs, packaging materials, expected labor hours, and standard yield/wastage % for every processed food SKU.
* **Daily Production Board (Kanban):** Visual tablet interface for the Production Manager showing `Queued Batches`, `In-Progress (Cooking/Processing/Packing)`, and `Completed`.


* **Floor Labor Allocation Log:** Simple daily headcount tracker logging attendance and shift output across the 10–15 factory employees.



### 6.6 Logistics, Dispatch & Delivery

* **Trip & Load Consolidation:** Groups confirmed orders by geographical route and vehicle capacity across the 3–4 drivers.


* **Driver Mobile App:** Offline-capable Android interface providing turn-by-turn stop lists, customer contact buttons, item counts to unload, crate/container return tracking, and fuel/toll expense logging with receipt photo capture.



### 6.7 Collections, Receivables & Day-End Accounting

* **Real-Time Receivables Sub-Ledger:** Instant invoicing upon dispatch; live tracking of `Total Outstanding`, `Overdue Amount`, and `Unsettled Field Collections`.


* **Custodian Wallet Reconciliation (EOD Fix):** Tracks exactly how much physical cash or checks each driver and collection staff member is holding at any minute of the day.


* **10-Minute EOD Closing Checklist:** Automated screen showing:
1. All Trips Closed? (Yes/No)
2. All Field Cash Handed Over & Counted? (Yes/No)
3. Any Uninvoiced Deliveries? (Yes/No)
4. Daily Net Cash Position & Bank Deposit Slip generated.





### 6.8 Reporting & Executive Control Tower

Mr. X receives three structured views instead of making dozens of phone calls:

1. **Live Pulse Header (Mobile Top Bar):** `Today's Sales (₹)` | `Gross Margin (%)` | `Cash Collected Today (₹)` | `Pending Exceptions (Count)`.
2. **Operational Status Matrix:** Live progress bars for `Orders Pending Dispatch`, `Factory Batches Running`, `Trucks on Road (Stop X of Y)`, and `Supplier Shortfalls`.
3. **Weekly/Monthly Strategic Analytics:** Customer Profitability Ranking, Supplier Fill-Rate & Price Inflation Report, Factory Yield & Wastage Trend, and AR Aging (0–7, 8–15, 16–30, 30+ days).

* **Why Offline-First Mobile (SQLite / WatermelonDB Sync)?** Drivers and collection staff travel across multiple areas where cellular connectivity may drop. The mobile app caches the assigned Trip Manifest and Customer Ledger locally, allowing drivers to capture ePOD signatures and cash receipts offline and auto-syncing the moment connectivity returns.


* **Why PostgreSQL with ACID Compliance?** Inventory reservation across sites and financial sub-ledger postings (Customer AR + Agent Cash Custody) require strict database transaction isolation (`SERIALIZABLE` / `READ COMMITTED` with row locking) to prevent double-allocation of food stock or missing financial entries.

### 7.3 Security, Governance & Fraud Prevention

1. **Site-Scoped Data Isolation:** Row-Level Security (RLS) ensures warehouse staff and production managers only see operational queues relevant to their assigned location, while Mr. X has unrestricted cross-site visibility.


2. **Append-Only Financial & Stock Ledgers:** Users cannot `UPDATE` or `DELETE` rows in `stock_ledger_txns` or `collection_receipts`. Corrections require a formal reversal entry, creating an unbreakable audit trail that eliminates "missed entries" or silent manipulation.


3. **Price & Margin Masking:** Factory employees, drivers, and warehouse staff see only product names, batch numbers, and quantities—unit prices, supplier rates, and customer profit margins are hidden at the API serialization layer.


4. **Automated Geo-Tagging & Timestamps:** Every mobile ePOD delivery confirmation and field cash collection captures automatic GPS coordinates and device timestamps to verify field staff presence at customer locations.

### 7.4 Key External Integrations

* **WhatsApp Business API (Primary Customer/Supplier Channel):** Because customers and suppliers in the food trade are accustomed to phone/messaging communication, NeoCore automatically sends PDF Order Confirmations, Dispatch Alerts (with driver contact), Tax Invoices, and Instant Payment Receipts to customers via WhatsApp—drastically cutting inbound *"Where is my order?"* or *"Did you record my payment?"* calls to Mr. X.


* **Accounting Software Bridge (Tally Prime / Zoho Books / QuickBooks):** NeoCore acts as the operational front-end and sub-ledger, exporting verified daily sales invoices, purchase bills, and reconciled collection vouchers via automated nightly XML/API sync to the statutory tax accounting system.

### 9.2 Comprehensive Testing & Validation Strategy

1. **Conference Room Pilot (CRP) — Scenario Simulation:** Before writing production code, walk Mr. X, the Production Manager, and lead collection staff through clickable wireframes using **Scenarios A through F** from the case study to validate screen simplicity.


2. **Parallel Run for Financial Reconciliation (7 Days):** During Week 6, run the Mobile Collection App and EOD Cash Handover module in parallel with Mr. X's existing manual end-of-day account review. Verify that NeoCore catches 100% of transactions and highlights any entries missed by the manual process.


3. **Field Offline & Low-Connectivity Stress Test:** Test the Driver and Collector mobile apps in airplane mode and weak 2G/3G zones to verify that offline ePOD photos, partial delivery adjustments, and cash receipts queue cleanly in local SQLite storage and sync without duplication upon reconnection.
4. **Guardrail & Penetration Testing:** Attempt to bypass Floor Prices, Credit Limits, and Cash Custody balances using non-owner accounts to verify that RBAC permissions and database triggers block unauthorized actions.

### 9.3 Adoption Risks & Practical Mitigation Plan

| Risk Category | Specific Operational Risk in Neo's Context | Concrete Mitigation Strategy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Owner Habit Regression** | Customers and drivers are habituated to calling Mr. X’s personal mobile number directly, bypassing the new system and staff.

 | **Call Redirection & Protocol:** Mr. X greets routine callers with: *"I've approved your price band on the desk—transferring you to [Coordinator] to lock your dispatch slot immediately."* Customers quickly learn the Order Desk is faster for routine confirmation. |
| **Field Staff Digital Literacy** | Drivers (3–4) and factory staff may struggle with complex ERP screens or English-heavy text inputs.

 | **Zero-Typing / Local Language UI:** The Driver and Factory apps use large, color-coded buttons, local language labels, voice-note attachments for exceptions, and numeric keypads only. |
| **Shadow Pricing / Cash Leakage** | Collection staff might collect cash from a customer and delay logging it in the app to use the float personally.

 | **Customer WhatsApp Loop:** Customers are informed that only digital WhatsApp receipts generated instantly by NeoCore are valid proof of payment. Additionally, any delivery marked `COD` blocks trip closure until cash is logged. |
| **Master Data Setup Fatigue** | Defining exact BOM recipes for processed foods or initial customer opening balances stalls go-live. | **Progressive Data Enrichment:** Launch Phase 1 with Simple Item SKUs and verified Customer AR balances. Layer in multi-level BOM recipes in Phase 3 once daily order/collection habits are established. |
